🌍 Where visitors are

Each dot is a place people opened Constructa from in the selected period; dot area is proportional to visitors. Location comes from the browser's time zone — no IP lookup and no third-party geo service — so it is a region, not an address: everyone on Asia/Kolkata shares one dot. Drag the globe to spin it, or click a row to fly to it.

📊 By source

Landing visits are browser sessions on the landing page. People columns count unique emails, grouped by the source each person signed up through (or first arrived from).

👤 Users

One row per email with activity in the selected period. Activity counts are for that period. Click a row to see everything the person did. Copy new emails copies only accounts created (signed up) in the selected period and filters.
